**0:00–0:08 · Hook.** Full-screen landing. Point at the title and the pitch line.
> "Slice NAIRA. Touchless, accessible gaming, and fun physical therapy. No controller. Just your finger."

**0:08–0:18 · Camera.** Click PLAY WITH CAMERA. The webcam fades in behind the grid.
> "Runs fully in the browser. MediaPipe tracks the index fingertip. Frames never leave the device."
Wave three times. The calibration meter fills and the game unlocks.

**0:18–0:35 · Gameplay.** Level 1, Hello World. Slice a few fruit so the purple trail and the splits show. Land a PERFECT CUT (+₦500) and a 3-fruit swipe (COMBO x3 +₦1,500).
> "Juice everywhere: split halves, particles, screen shake. Naira counts up in the wallet."

**0:35–0:45 · Stakes.** Level 2 (Compile). Hit a bomb on purpose: lives drop, screen shakes, red flash.
> "Bombs cost lives. Missed fruit doesn't. Levels add speed, bombs, ice and frenzy. Ten levels plus endless mode."

**0:45–0:52 · Retention.** Open DAILY. Show the same-seed challenge, the login streak flame, and the missions.
> "Same seed for everyone every day. Login streak and missions bring people back. Local top 10, backend-ready."

**0:52–0:60 · Rehab close.** Stats → Rehab mode. Show reach bars and the session chart.
> "Rehab mode: slower, bigger fruit, and every rep and range-of-motion number is logged for therapists. Touchless, accessible, and fun. That's SLICE NAIRA."
